## Beta and full-game tracking

The safest checklist starts by separating build-specific objectives.

- Open Beta contains nine listed achievements.
- Do not assume beta achievement progress transfers to the formal release list.
- Record the build and date beside every achievement checklist.

## Missable-looking objectives

Exploration achievements are the most likely to be delayed by a rushed route.

- Search Beacons and hidden areas before leaving each region.
- Check Tar Golem and collection-related objectives against your route.
- Revisit Shell, weapon, and trial locations before the final boss push.

## 100% route planning

A low-spoiler route keeps completion work manageable.

- Finish automatic story objectives first, then clean up Shell and weapon tasks.
- Use a save checkpoint before optional boss attempts when the release rules are confirmed.
- Beta community estimates of about four hours are not a formal-game completion promise.
