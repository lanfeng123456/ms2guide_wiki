## Edition comparison

The premium is primarily about cosmetics and earlier access.

- Standard example: $49.99 in the US store.
- Devout example: $59.99 in the US store.
- Prices vary by region and store, so use the local listing at checkout.

## What Devout includes

Keep permanent included content separate from pre-order bonuses.

- The base game is included.
- All eight playable Shells receive an Obsidian skin set.
- Skeletal and Obsidian Harbinger skins are pre-order rewards, not the same as the Shell skin set.

## Is the upgrade worth it?

Devout does not change the combat rules or unlock a stronger Shell.

- Choose it for Advanced Access and cosmetic collection value.
- On Xbox, the Devout Upgrade requires ownership of the base game.
- Players who only want gameplay content can stay with Standard without losing the campaign.
