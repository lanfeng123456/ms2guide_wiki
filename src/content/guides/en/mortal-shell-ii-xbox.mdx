## Xbox release and editions

The base release date and the premium early-access date are different.

- Standard Edition releases August 20, 2026.
- Devout Advanced Access begins August 17, 2026.
- The supported hardware family is Xbox Series X|S.

## Finding Open Beta

A full-name search is more reliable than a generic beta query.

- Search Mortal Shell II Open Beta in the Xbox store or Xbox app.
- If the card is missing, open the product's editions and related entries.
