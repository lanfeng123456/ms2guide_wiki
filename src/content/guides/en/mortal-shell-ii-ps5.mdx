## Release and edition choices

The PS5 store concept page groups several products together.

- Standard Edition releases August 20, 2026.
- Devout Edition Advanced Access begins August 17, 2026.
- Open Beta can appear under Editions or the three-dot menu on the concept page.

## PS5 features

Store badges describe supported features, not exact performance targets.

- Single-player and offline play are listed.
- DualSense vibration and PS5 Pro Enhanced are listed.
- There is no confirmed PS4 version; do not promise backward-generation support.

## If the beta is missing

The listing hierarchy is the most common source of confusion.

- Open the product concept page rather than only searching the full-game title.
- Check Editions and the three-dot menu for the free beta entry.
- Confirm the account region and store age requirements before assuming the listing is gone.
