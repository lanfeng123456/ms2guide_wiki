## Switch products on PSN

The beta may not appear as the default product card.

- Open the official product concept page.
- Use Editions or the three-dot menu to select Open Beta.
- Standard and Devout are paid products with different release timing.

## Region and PS Plus notes

Store access can be region-specific rather than a game-wide rule.

- Open Beta is a free entry.
- Some regions, including Germany reports, may ask for PS Plus because of local age-rating rules.
- The PSN listing is PS5 only; do not write PS4 compatibility without an official update.

## When the listing is missing

Work through account and product context before reinstalling the console.

- Check the account region and age settings.
- Search the complete name Mortal Shell II Open Beta.
- Reopen the concept page and inspect Editions after the store refreshes.
