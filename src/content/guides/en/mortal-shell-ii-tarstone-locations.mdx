## Tarstone system

Treat Tarstones as build components, not isolated collectibles.

- Pair a Tarstone with the Shell's defensive and offensive identity.
- Check how the effect changes Resolve generation or spending.
- Record the weapon and sidearm used when testing a combination.

## Known beta route clues

These are useful leads, not final coordinates.

- Shrike Stone is associated with an alternate path in the second swamp-region area.
- Follow the explosive green stick totems as a route clue.
- Support Tarstones can appear behind buildings, in three-offering chests, or after trials.

## Avoiding false certainty

Beta map changes are expected during launch preparation.

- Serpent Stone appears in a Gameplay Reveal but was reported absent from the beta.
- Do not publish beta coordinates as formal-release coordinates without retesting.
- A missing chest can indicate an offering, trial, or progression condition rather than a bug.
