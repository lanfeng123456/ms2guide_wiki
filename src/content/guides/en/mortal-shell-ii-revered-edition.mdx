## Collector contents

Revered is built around physical memorabilia rather than gameplay power.

- Physical artbook.
- Steelcase.
- Fine art prints and digital additional content.

## Platform and availability

Check the edition type before comparing it with digital stores.

- The confirmed physical collector platform is PS5.
- It is distinct from the digital Devout Edition.
- The official site indicates that most regions are sold out.

## Pre-order rewards

Cosmetic rewards should be listed separately from the box contents.

- Pre-order buyers can receive two exclusive Harbinger skins.
- Stock status is not a guarantee that pre-order rewards are still available.
- Use official retailer pages for current inventory rather than unverified resale claims.
